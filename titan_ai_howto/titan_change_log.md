# Titan Change Log — How-To Guide
Owner: Pratik (PD) · App: Titan Change Log (titan-change-log.anvil.app) · Updated: 2026-08-20 (v1.3.0)

## What this app is for
The Change Log is where the team writes down changes made to Titan's systems, apps, equipment, and processes — one entry per change. It gives everyone a single place to see what changed, how big a deal it was, who asked for it, who owns it, and whether it's done, instead of that living in someone's head or an email.

## Who uses it
The production-control / IT group, and eventually the floor. It opens in a web browser on an office PC (no install) — go to the app link. The left-hand menu has **Home** and **Change Log**.

## How do I …

### How do I start?
The first thing you see is a **"Who's asking?"** box. Pick your name from the list — or, if you're not in the list yet, type it in the box underneath — and press **Start**. That's who the app records as you for everything you do this visit. Your name shows top-right as **"Asking as: …"**; press **switch** there to hand the screen to someone else.

### How do I log a new change?
1. On the Change Log page, click **New change** (in the bar above the list).
2. A box opens. Fill in:
   - **Description** — what's changing, in a sentence. (Required — it won't save blank.)
   - **Category** — the area it affects: Database, Application, Process, Equipment, Documentation, or Other.
   - **Impact** — how big: Low, Medium, High, or Critical.
   - **Status** — new items start as **Proposed**. (If the Status dropdown is greyed out for you, that's normal — see "Who can change status?" below.)
   - **Owner** — pick from the list who will be responsible for delivering it, or leave **Unassigned**.
   - **Requested by** is filled in automatically — it's whoever you picked on the "Who's asking?" screen.
3. Click **Save**. The entry appears at the top of the list with its own ID like **CL-8**.

### How do I find a change or filter the list?
- Use the **Status** dropdown (e.g. show only "Approved") and/or the **Category** dropdown, then click **Search**.
- Or type a word from the description, or a person's name (requester or owner), in the **search box** and click **Search**.
- To see everything again, set the dropdowns back to **All statuses** / **All categories**, clear the search box, and click **Search**.

### How do I update a change?
1. Find the entry and click the **pencil (Edit) icon** at the right end of its row.
2. Change what you need and click **Save**.

### Who can change status? (Approved, In Progress, Implemented, Cancelled)
Anyone can log a request, but only **owners** — currently Pratik and Dave — can move an entry's Status. If you're not an owner, the Status dropdown is greyed out with a note saying so; everything else (description, category, impact, owner) you can still edit. Need a status moved? Ask an owner.

### How do I read the Status colors?
- **Proposed** (grey) — suggested, not yet approved.
- **Approved** (blue) — signed off, okay to proceed.
- **In Progress** (amber) — being worked on now.
- **Implemented** (green) — done and live.
- **Cancelled** (red) — decided against; not happening.

### Does anyone get notified?
Yes — when a new change is logged or a status changes, the app owner (Pratik) automatically gets a text message naming the requester and owner. You don't have to do anything. Editing only the description or owner (without changing status) stays silent.

## When something looks wrong
- **It's asking me "Who's asking?" again** — your session timed out (it happens after the browser sits idle). Just pick your name and carry on; nothing was lost.
- **The Status dropdown is greyed out** — you're not an owner; that's by design. Ask Pratik or Dave to move the status.
- **My new entry isn't showing** — the list is probably still filtered. Set Status and Category back to "All…", clear the search box, and click **Search**.
- **"Description is required"** — the Description box was empty; type what's changing and save again.
- **I ended up on the wrong screen** — click **Change Log** in the left menu.
