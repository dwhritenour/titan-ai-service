# Titan AI — How-To Guide
Owner: Dave · App: Titan AI (titan-ai.anvil.app) · Updated: 2026-08-07

## What this app is for
Titan AI is the company's AI assistant. Ask it plain-English questions and it answers from live Nexus data (work orders, picks, inventory, transfers, min/max, sales orders) and from Titan's product literature (spec sheets, the price list, marketing documents). It is strictly **read-only** — it can't approve, change, or delete anything; decisions stay in the apps.

## Who uses it
Anyone at Titan. Open it from the **✦ Titan AI** button in the other apps or at titan-ai.anvil.app.

## How do I …

### How do I start?
Pick your name on the "Who's asking?" screen (or type it) and press **Start**. Then type a question and press the **↑** button — or tap one of the suggested question chips.

### What can I ask?
Operational questions ("What work orders are due this week?", "What are the current open picks?", "Which SKUs are below min at Starlite?"), product questions ("What's the face-to-face on a 2-inch YS61-CS?", "Which strainers are ASME Class 250?"), pricing ("List price on a 3-inch CV91?"), and combinations ("Do we have stock on anything rated 600 PSI?"). Follow-ups work — it remembers the conversation until you press **+ New chat**.

### How do I get data as a spreadsheet?
Ask for it: "Export all below-min SKUs to Excel." A **📄 … is ready** chip appears in the chat — press **⬇ Download Excel**. Exports contain the complete result set, not just what was shown in chat.

### How do I know if the answer is right?
Press **How I got this** under any answer to see exactly which lookups and queries produced it. Answers cite spec sheets by page, and price answers always say "list price" and name the price-list version. If an answer is wrong, press **👎** — that feedback genuinely improves the assistant. Wait for the grey "thinking" lines to finish; most answers take 5–20 seconds.

### What does it NOT do?
It won't approve transfers, release work orders, change min/max, or take any action — it will point you to the right app instead. It doesn't know the public internet — only Titan's data and documents. Prices it quotes are LIST prices — confirm with sales before quoting a customer.

## When something looks wrong
- **Stuck on "Titan AI is thinking…" for minutes:** the answering service is likely restarting — your question is queued, not lost; it answers when the service returns. Tell Dave if it persists.
- **It says it can't find something you know exists:** rephrase with the exact SKU or model number, and give it a 👎 with the question — that's how gaps get fixed.
- **An answer contradicts an app screen:** trust the app; the assistant's data can lag e21 by ~15 minutes. Report it with 👎.
